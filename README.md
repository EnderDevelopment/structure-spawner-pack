# Minecraft Bedrock Structure Spawner Behavior Pack

Structure Spawner is a Minecraft Bedrock behavior pack for spawning custom builds and vanilla structures with in-game functions. It includes a starter house, watchtower, and farm garden, plus vanilla structures placed with Bedrock’s `/place structure` command. Most structures cost 1 iron ingot; the End City costs 10.

## Install

1. Copy the `Structure Spawner` pack folder into your world’s `behavior_packs` folder.
2. Open the world’s settings and enable Structure Spawner under Behavior Packs.
3. Load the world, enable cheats, and enter the dimension suited to the structure you want to spawn.

## Use

1. Stand where you want the structure to appear and make sure you have enough iron ingots.
2. Run a function in chat. For example, `/function structure_spawner/house` spawns the starter house.
3. Use `/function structure_spawner/help` to see the available functions.

Available functions:

- `/function structure_spawner/house` — furnished starter house
- `/function structure_spawner/tower` — stone watchtower
- `/function structure_spawner/garden` — fenced crop garden
- `/function structure_spawner/village` — vanilla village
- `/function structure_spawner/shipwreck` — vanilla shipwreck
- `/function structure_spawner/monument` — vanilla ocean monument
- `/function structure_spawner/bastion` — vanilla bastion remnant; use in the Nether
- `/function structure_spawner/fortress` — vanilla Nether fortress; use in the Nether
- `/function structure_spawner/mansion` — vanilla woodland mansion
- `/function structure_spawner/ancient_city` — vanilla ancient city
- `/function structure_spawner/endcity` — vanilla End City; costs 10 iron ingots

The pack places structures at your location. Choose a suitable dimension and a large, clear area, since structures may replace blocks within their footprint. Vanilla structures use the game’s built-in structure placement and preserve their vanilla loot behavior. Requires Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21 or newer.
